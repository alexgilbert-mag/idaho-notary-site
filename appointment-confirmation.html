All Set! Your Appointment is Confirmed

Thank you for giving MAG Notary Services the opportunity to assist with your notary needs. Here is what you should expect before your signing session takes place:

01

Confirmation Email

You should receive an email shortly confirming the date and time of our signing session. Please make sure it is what you expected, and reach out right away via email if we need to correct any errors.

02

Document Upload Request

You will receive a separate email containing a request to upload the documents for which you require notarization, as well as contact information for the signers. This link will take you to the Secure Upload Center, which uses SSL-encryption to ensure your data is protected.

03

Document Preparation & Review

Upon receipt of the uploaded documentation, our team will review the documentation for thorough completion and to verify all required signers. At this point, each signer will be issued an invitation to the signing session.

04

Identity Verification

At the time of our scheduled appointment, please have the following information available: photo identification, your mobile device, and/or the device in which you will participate in the signing (must have a working webcam and microphone - mobile devices will work!)

05

Signing & Document Copies

Our team will walk you through the signing process, and afterwards you will receive the completed notarized copies of all your documentation. This will be sent directly to the email address of each signer. You're all set!

Your request is in good hands.
